About Me

Creator of GraalGuide

azrxc

Graal Enthusiast & Community Contributor

Welcome to GraalGuide. This website is one of my personal projects, created to serve as a reliable fan resource where players can easily find guides, rules, and tips about Graal Era.

I have been playing Graal since my primary school years, with more than ten years of experience and over 10,000 hours of playtime. Back then, I often struggled with English in school and usually scored poorly in exams. But through playing Graal and communicating with players from around the world, my English began to improve naturally. The game gave me not only entertainment, but also the chance to learn, connect, and grow.

During the COVID-19 lockdowns in Malaysia, Graal became an even bigger part of my life. While the world felt quiet, the game was alive with activity. I spent countless hours exploring, trading, and chatting, and it was during that time that I met many friends who were kind, supportive, and fun to be around. Some of them live in the same country as me, yet we've never met in real life, but we're still in contact to this day, which shows how strong the connections in this community can be.

Even though I no longer play as often, Graal has shaped my journey in many ways, and I wanted to give something back to the community that has given me so much. This site may be simple since I am not a professional developer, but I am committed to improving it and keeping it useful for players.

Thank you for visiting and supporting this project.

- azrxc

My Graal Journey

My journey with Graal began in primary school and has continued for over a decade. What started as a simple game quickly became a significant part of my life, helping me develop language skills, make international friends, and create lasting memories.

During the pandemic, Graal became my sanctuary - a place where I could connect with others when the real world felt isolated. The friendships I formed during that time remain some of the most meaningful connections I've made through gaming.

About GraalGuide

GraalGuide was created to give back to the community that has given me so much. The site features comprehensive guides, trading resources, achievement tracking, and other tools to help players navigate the world of Graal Era.

As someone who isn't a professional developer, I've focused on creating a clean, functional website that serves the community's needs. I'm always open to feedback and suggestions for how to improve the site.
