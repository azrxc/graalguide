Event Coins Guide

Introduction to Event Coins (EC)

Updated on January 1, 2026

Event Coins (EC) are a special currency in Graal Era used to buy exclusive rewards from event shops. This guide will show you all the methods to earn EC through events, achievements, and daily activities.

Event House & Event Bots

Event House

Join when admins host official events:

  • Team events usually reward only 0.1 EC for the winning team
  • Tag Team Spar (TTS): Only the final winning player gets 1 EC
  • Races, chance events, and skill-based events reward 1 EC if you win

Event Bot

Enable Event Bot in your settings to receive event invitations:

  • Go into settings and enable Event Bot notifications
  • When the bot invites you, join the event quickly
  • Winning the event rewards you with 1 full EC

Plasma & Laser Tag MVP

Plasma (PvP)

Become the MVP in plasma fights to earn EC:

  • Focus on getting the highest score in matches
  • MVP typically receives 0.1-0.3 EC depending on performance
  • Play during peak hours for more competitive matches

Laser Tag

Be the MVP in laser tag matches to earn EC:

  • Similar to plasma, aim for the highest score
  • MVP rewards range from 0.1-0.3 EC
  • Practice aiming and movement to improve your skills

Secret Achievements

Hidden EC Rewards

Many secret or hidden achievements reward EC pieces:

  • Most achievements give 0.1 EC pieces rather than full EC
  • When you collect 0.3 EC pieces, it becomes 1 full EC
  • Example: 3 secret achievements (0.1 + 0.1 + 0.1) = 1 EC

Finding Secret Achievements

The easiest way is to check YouTube videos or ask experienced players about which achievements are still available and how to complete them.

Daily & Weekly Quests

Regular EC Opportunities

Quests reset daily and weekly with EC rewards:

  • Some quests reward Event Coin pieces (e.g., 0.1 EC)
  • Check your quest log regularly for EC opportunities
  • Focus on completing quests with EC rewards first

Hi5 Quest Strategy

For the "Hi5 Quest," go to Start area right after daily reset — you'll find many players doing it at the same time, making it easier to complete quickly.

EC Collection Tips & Strategies

Be Consistent

Regular participation is key to accumulating EC over time. Even small amounts add up!

Prioritize High-Value Activities

Focus on activities that give full EC first, then work on EC pieces:

  • Event wins (1 EC)
  • MVP in Plasma/Laser Tag (0.1-0.3 EC)
  • Daily/Weekly quests with EC rewards
  • Secret achievements (0.1 EC each)

Join the Community

Connect with other players to learn about event schedules, achievement tips, and team up for better results in team events.

Conclusion

Earning Event Coins requires persistence and knowledge of all available methods. By participating in events, completing achievements, and staying consistent with daily activities, you can accumulate enough EC to purchase exclusive items from event shops. Happy hunting!
