Last updated: September 9, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Personal information you provide

When you contact us (via email or contact form), or sign up for newsletters (if applicable), we may collect your name and email address.

Non-personal / usage data

We automatically collect certain data about how you use the site, such as:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type & version
  • Which pages you visit and how long you stay
  • Referring URL (which page you came from)

Cookies

We may use cookies or similar tools to store information on your device or record your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but that might reduce some functionalities.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your messages or feedback.
  • To improve the site: content, navigation, performance.
  • To monitor site usage, errors, and analytics (e.g. how many visitors, page views).
  • If there are features that require email/newsletter, we may use your email only for sending you that content (with opt-out possible if applicable).

3. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties for marketing.

We might use third-party services/tools (e.g. analytics, hosting), which may have access to non-personal data.

If required by law, your data may be disclosed (e.g. legal requests).

4. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of online transmission or storage is 100% secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

5. Your Rights

  • You can request access to any personal data we have about you.
  • You can ask us to correct or update your data.
  • If applicable, you can opt-out of marketing or newsletters.
  • If you want us to delete your data, you can contact us and we'll try to accommodate (unless we're legally required to keep it).

6. Children's Privacy

Our site is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected such data, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. External Links

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies when leaving GraalGuide.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Effective Date" at the top. We encourage you to check back periodically to see any changes.
