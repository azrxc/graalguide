Submit Your Creation

Made a head, body, hat, shield, or sword you're proud of? Send it in and it might show up in the community galleries.

How this works

  • Fill out the form below and attach your image (PNG or GIF, 3MB max).
  • It goes into a review queue, not straight onto the site. We check for trademarked characters, duplicates, and anything that's not actually your work.
  • If it's approved, it gets added to the matching community gallery automatically, usually within an hour.
  • You can submit up to 5 items a day.
